Rhapsody carries the lowest incremental collision exposure of the five and is the better starting point for clearance if this posture is pursued. A sixth candidate, Maestro, was considered and rejected outright — it is already the internal codename for Symphony's planned MEO-regime platform, which would create a direct clash with the parent's own product line rather than a merely thematic one.

Channel, not a segment — Creative & Media Agencies

Most rows above transact through an agency, not direct to the end brand. One holding-company relationship can pitch the Studio Arm into a dozen client accounts at once — the fastest lever for compressing the slowest-closing segments (Fashion Maisons, Spirits & Hospitality). Screen the agency's client roster, not just the agency.

New segment — Deep-Tech & Life-Sciences Tenant Storytelling

Symphony's own commercial R&D tenants — the AWS / Catalyst Data Centers / Bio-Techne-shaped customer — already fly payloads on the platform. Selling them documentary capture of their own orbital work is a 1–3 month warm upsell with near-zero incremental collision risk, not a cold luxury pitch. Best served as a distinct Spectra product line, priced as technical communications, not media.

Launch Spectra. Hold Monolith in reserve. Do not launch Aria as written.

Spectra closes fastest with the buyer segment ranked highest on attractiveness (Tech & AI Brands), carries no weakness that isn't solvable with a filed modifier, and fits the October 4, 2026 NYC Studio Launch timeline. Monolith's firewall is marginally stronger and should be activated once Spectra has proven the model with the slower-closing heritage-luxury segment. Aria's soloist narrative is the most resonant of the three — and the one whose own name reopens the "Adagio" trail the Studio Arm exists to close.

Open risk: the $60B figure is a 2030–2035 category-wide estimate, not a Studio Arm revenue target — the platform's own fleet economics bound a realistic SOM to roughly $15–25M ARR by 2030. Every external-facing Studio Arm document should describe the $60B as the market this business plays adjacent to, never as the revenue it is sized to capture.
